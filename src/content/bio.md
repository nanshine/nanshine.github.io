I am a master's student in Statistics at the School of Data Science, Fudan University (2024&ndash;2027), advised by Prof. Zhongyu Wei at the Data Intelligence and Social Computing Lab (Fudan DISC). I received my B.E. in Software Engineering from Tongji University in 2024.

My research interests focus on large language models, including **LLM agents**, **medical LLMs**, and **LLM safety**. I am currently an intern at Alibaba ATH (formerly Tongyi Lab).
